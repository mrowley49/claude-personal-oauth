Privacy Policy

Claude Personal is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, to access the owner's own Google account.

  • Data accessed through Google APIs stays on the owner's computer and is used only to carry out tasks the owner requests.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally with owner-only file permissions and can be revoked at any time from Google Account settings, under Security, Third-party access.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
